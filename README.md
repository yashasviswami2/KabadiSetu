# ♻️ Kabadi Setu

### AI-Powered Bridge from Informal E-Waste Collection to Formal Recycling

Kabadi Setu is a smart recycling platform designed to connect **households, informal waste collectors, and authorized recyclers** through a single digital ecosystem.

The platform uses **AI-based material classification, transparent price discovery, recycler comparison, pickup requests, and digital traceability** to make the recycling process more organized, transparent, and accessible.

---

## 🚀 Problem Statement

The current recycling ecosystem faces several challenges:

- Informal waste collectors are disconnected from formal recycling networks.
- There is limited transparency in material pricing.
- The collection and selling process is fragmented.
- Waste movement is difficult to trace.
- E-waste may be handled through unsafe informal practices.
- Collectors have limited access to verified recycler information.
- Poor connectivity and limited digital literacy create adoption barriers.

Kabadi Setu aims to address these gaps by creating a digital bridge between the informal and formal recycling ecosystem.
